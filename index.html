          The map's own GPS not working?

          Only needed for the blue dot, follow-me and the compass while the map is open. OwnTracks works without it.

          1. iPhone settings: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services: on. Scroll down to Safari Websites → While Using the App, and turn on Precise Location.

          2. Safari, for this site: tap aA (or the icon left of the address) → Website Settings → Location → Allow.

          3. Close and open again: swipe the app or Safari away and open the map again, then tap the position button. Answer Allow when it asks about location.

          The compass is asked for on the next tap on the map, after the position works.
